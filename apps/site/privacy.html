Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Adelie (“Adelie”, “we”, “us”) is a marketing analytics platform available at adelie.space. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Profile data (optional): details you choose to add in personal settings — job title, photo, contact email, phone, messenger and LinkedIn contacts, time zone and working hours. They are visible only to members of workspaces you belong to, and you can change or remove them at any time.
  • Connected platform data: when you connect a social media account or an online shop (for example Pinterest or Etsy), and only with your explicit authorization, we receive profile information, your content (such as Pins, boards and shop listings) and analytics metrics for that account. For a connected shop we also receive order and sales data to measure marketing results; we do not store buyers' contact or address details.
  • Public market data: to research trends and competition we use publicly available data provided by platform APIs, such as public marketplace listings (prices, categories, tags) and aggregated search trends. It does not identify individual visitors.
  • Technical data: session information, IP address and browser type, used to keep your account secure.

Read access by default

For social media accounts Adelie requests read-only permissions and never publishes, edits or deletes content. For a connected online shop, Adelie may create or update your own listings only when you explicitly ask it to (for example, publishing a listing draft you reviewed); it never deletes listings and never changes orders.

How we use data

We use your data solely to provide the service to you: to display analytics, keep a history of your metrics and generate reports and insights within your workspace. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with third parties, except infrastructure providers that help us operate the service (such as Cloudflare for network delivery) or where required by law.

Storage and security

Access tokens for connected platforms are encrypted at rest and are never exposed to your browser. Access to data is limited to members of your workspace.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect a platform account at any time; its access tokens are deleted immediately. You can request deletion of your Adelie account and all associated data by contacting us — we will complete deletion within 30 days. We also delete data when required by the policies of the connected platform.

Third-party platforms

Data obtained from connected platforms is also subject to their terms and privacy policies, such as the Pinterest Privacy Policy and the Etsy Privacy Policy.

The term “Etsy” is a trademark of Etsy, Inc. This application uses the Etsy API but is not endorsed or certified by Etsy, Inc.

Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be announced in the product or by email.
